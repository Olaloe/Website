What we do

Our approach

We combine somatic practices, mindfulness, games, and guided group reflections to build community and encourage people to take an active role in their social environment.

💚

Self-care & well-being

Supporting self-awareness and emotional health through body-based and somatic practices, mindfulness, and guided reflections.

🤝

Community building

Creating safe, inclusive spaces where people feel a sense of belonging and connection — to each other and to their social environment.

🌱

Nature & sustainability

Fostering a respectful and caring relationship with the natural world, promoting sustainable attitudes through empathy rather than guilt.

🌍

International cooperation

Building lasting networks of partnership through Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps to co-create initiatives across Europe.

Who we are

Meet Olaloe

Olaloe is a newly established organisation founded in December 2025 by Irene and Sara. It was created with great enthusiasm and a strong commitment to sharing practices through experience-based learning.

After several years of involvement in Erasmus+ projects, we decided to take the next step and create our own space, building on what we learned about non-formal education, participatory methods, and community building in international environments.

We stay curious and open to improving ourselves, because we don't claim to know everything — but we are willing to learn. We aim to create safe, inclusive spaces where people can grow, take responsibility, and explore more sustainable ways of living together.

Irene Hortal Gil
Project Manager · Legal Representative

Clinical psychologist with experience in mental health promotion and group facilitation. Since 2018 she has worked with young people, leading workshops on emotional wellbeing, body awareness, and self-care, integrating somatic and nature-based practices.

Sara Ghivarello
Project Manager · Contact Person

Involved in youth work since 2017. Experience in community engagement through volunteering with Red Cross Youth and co-creating a local social space, facilitating activities focused on dialogue, sports, and environmental awareness.
